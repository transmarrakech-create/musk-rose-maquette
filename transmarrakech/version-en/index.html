Ourika Valley day trip from Marrakech

One hour of road, and the air changes. The Ourika valley climbs south of Marrakech, or Marrakesh if that is the spelling on your plane ticket, into the first ridges of the Atlas, following a river that keeps the whole valley cool while the city bakes. That short distance is the whole point: you leave your riad after breakfast and you are walking beside running water before mid-morning. Where the road stops

The valley starts about 30 km out of the city and the road ends at the douar of Setti Fatma, the last point a vehicle reaches. After that you walk. The climb to the first of the seven waterfalls is short, and the path is busy with people doing exactly the same thing. Berber houses hang on the slope above it. Down by the river, families set out tables and cook tagines from vegetables picked that morning, and there are mules for anyone who would rather ride the lower stretch than walk it.

Ourika has held on to its mountain routine longer than most valleys within reach of the city, and that is a good part of what people come up to see. It also runs cooler than the plain, which is why Moroccan visitors crowd the riverside restaurants in summer alongside everyone else.
