Minibus and coach hire with driver in Marrakech

Trans Marrakech (Anaconda Tours - Trans Marrakech SARL) has run transport in Marrakech and across Morocco since 2007. Airport transfers, a private driver for the day, minibus and coach hire with driver, day trips, multi-day tours to the desert: you book from home, and you know the price before you get in. We are a licensed passenger transport company (Moroccan Ministry of Equipment and Transport, licence PCT/ATT.T/8/2012), rated 4.9 out of 5 by the travellers who booked before you. Marrakech (also spelled Marrakesh) is our home city and the base for everything we run.

What we do

Airport transfers and taxis

A driver meets you inside Marrakech Menara Airport with a name board and takes you straight to your hotel or riad. The price is fixed, set before you travel, and it is the price you pay: no night surcharge. If your flight is late, we follow the flight number and the driver waits. You pay nothing extra for the delay.

Minibus and coach hire with driver

A van, a 14 to 18-seat minibus or a coach, sized to your group, with a driver who knows the region. You set the day's programme and stop where you like. Fuel is either on you or included depending on the option; the driver and his expenses are always in the price.

Day trips from Marrakech

Ourika Valley, Imlil, the Ouzoud waterfalls, Essaouira, Ait Ben Haddou, the Agafay desert. You are picked up at your accommodation in the morning and brought back in the evening. The rate is per vehicle, never per person, whatever category you book.

Multi-day tours across Morocco

Two to five days towards Merzouga, Zagora, M'Hamid or the imperial cities. You choose the stops; we plan the roads, the driving times and the overnight stays. Book a ready-made route or a trip built around your own plan.

Why book with Trans Marrakech

  • A declared, licensed transport company (ministerial licence PCT/ATT.T/8/2012), operating in Marrakech since 2007.
  • A fixed price given before departure, net to pay, with no night supplement and nothing hidden.
  • A recent, air-conditioned fleet, from a van to a 48-seat coach. Wifi on board on request.
  • Drivers who know Marrakech and its roads. They will point you to a good restaurant or the right hour to visit a site, without standing in for the official guide that guided visits require.
  • 739 reviews rated 4.9 on Tripadvisor and Google, written by travellers who booked from abroad, the same way you will.

Book your transport

Tell us your route or your programme, your dates and how many passengers. We reply with a quote at a fixed price. Once the booking is confirmed, you receive the driver's name, the make of the vehicle and its registration.

Marrakech airport transfer

A driver meets you at Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) and takes you straight to your accommodation. The price is per vehicle, not per person, set in advance by destination zone. You book ahead, online or by phone, and the fare quoted when you book is the fare you pay.

How your arrival works

  • At the arrivals exit, the driver greets you with a name board.
  • We track your flight number: if the flight is late, the driver waits, at no extra charge.
  • You get into a recent, air-conditioned vehicle and head to your hotel or riad.
  • The price given when you book is the price paid: fixed, net, with no night surcharge.

Where we drop you

We cover every district of Marrakech and take you to your hotel, wherever it is:

  • Guéliz, Hivernage and the city centre
  • The Medina
  • The Palmeraie (up to 7 km, up to 15 km)
  • Targa
  • The Agdal tourist zone

The larger hotels of the Palmeraie, the Agdal and Hivernage are all served (Mandarin Oriental, Pullman, Riu Tikida and Iberostar among them). We also run the connections between Guéliz or the Medina and the Palmeraie.

Arriving as a group of 8 or more

A group of fifteen does not fit in a car or a standard van, and this is where most transfer services stop. For eight passengers and up we send a 14, 16 or 18-seat minibus, and the price stays per vehicle: the same fare whether the minibus is half full or full. Beyond the airport

The same service covers your work, event and private travel around Marrakech: meetings, conferences, seminars, sightseeing. You give us the route, the times and the number of passengers, and we send the quote.

Frequently asked questions

Does the driver wait if my flight is delayed?

Yes. We track your flight number and adjust the pick-up time. There is no extra charge for a delayed flight.

Do you charge per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. The price is the same whether the vehicle is half full or full.

Is it Marrakech or Marrakesh?

Both spellings are correct. Marrakech is the French spelling, used by easyJet, Viator and Tripadvisor; Marrakesh is common in English too. They are the same city, whose airport code is RAK.

Marrakech (also spelled Marrakesh) is where we are based, but our minibuses run the whole country.

Our fleet

Private driver in Marrakech, at your disposal

A driver at your disposal, by the half-day or longer

Book a driver for a half-day, a full day or several days running. He drives, parks and waits while you visit; you set the stops and the pace. One point worth stating plainly: a driver is not an official guide. He will get you to the site, suggest the right hour to go and a good place to eat, but the guided visit of a monument or a medina requires the licensed guide that Moroccan rules call for. That line is where his job ends and the guide's begins.

Booking your airport transfer online

For your transfer to or from Marrakech airport, fill in the booking form. Here is what each field is for:

  • Flight origin: the city or airport you fly from (Paris-Orly, Madrid, Toulouse, and so on).
  • Flight number: it lets us track your flight and see a delay coming.
  • Landing date and time: the driver is usually at the airport about 15 minutes before the time you give.
  • Your name: it goes on the board the driver holds at the arrivals exit.
  • Destination address: the name and address of your accommodation, so we price the run accurately.
  • Return date and time: when you would like the driver to collect you.
  • Return departure time: useful if we need to advise you to bring the pick-up forward because of traffic.

Something specific to ask? Note it in the message field.

Event transport since 2007

A team of coordinators, assistants and drivers, reachable 24/7, takes charge of your guests' and delegates' movements from arrival to departure. Wedding guest transport, conference and seminar shuttles, incentive convoys: the job is the same, get everyone to the right place at the right time.

What we work out before your event

An event runs well when people move cleanly between the pick-up and drop-off points. We prepare every stage in advance and factor in whatever can slow a journey on the day. In practice, we commit to:

  • checking each venue's access against the vehicle categories that can reach it;
  • calculating waiting times at pick-up and at the welcome;
  • scouting the lesser-known locations ahead of time;
  • reading the traffic by hour, weekend and holiday, and giving you the timings and safety margins to plan for.

A transport plan that fits your budget

From what you send us (guest numbers, locations, timings), we build a movement plan made for your event and your budget. You get the number of vehicles, the routes and the schedule, not a template.
